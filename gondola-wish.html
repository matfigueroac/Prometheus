GONDOLA WISH

Summary

GONDOLA WISH is an important piece of the Stargate package not because it proves that parapsychology worked, but because it shows something more concrete: in 1978 there were actors in the US military/intelligence apparatus who were already treating the issue as an OPSEC threat serious enough to require training, personnel and program continuity. The memo turns the psi = curiosidad marginal hypothesis into something else: psi = posible vector de inteligencia y vulnerabilidad de seguridad.

What does it actually contain?

The document is a bureaucratic memo, not an experiment. Its main function is:

  • formulate the problema
  • declare supuestos
  • summarize hechos relevantes
  • close with conclusiones
  • recommend action

In other words, it is a piece of programmatic justification. It does not try to convince with reproducible data; attempts to convince the chain of command that it is worth integrating the parapsychological threat into the operational security framework.

Memo structure

1. Problem

The formal stated problem is to integrate the threat of Soviet and Eastern European parapsychological intelligence collection into the all-source OPSEC scenario.

2. Assumptions

The memo is based on a series of strong assumptions:

  • the USSR would have real parapsychological research, classified and supported by defense/KGB
  • The program would have been active since the early 1960s.
  • The Soviets would be ahead of the US in a variety of critical methods and experiments.
  • paranormal abilities would have practical application, especially for intelligence
  • CIA would have used similar techniques successfully in offensive collection
  • SRI and other US actors have already demonstrated repeated operational utility

3. Facts bearing on the problem

Then the institutional concern lands:

  • no one had yet assessed the security impact of this threat
  • Possible targets: R&D of advanced weapons and high-level military organizations
  • the 902d MI Group would be the best unit to deal with the problem
  • specialized training with external civilian support would be needed
  • The final objective would be to have a small capability set to demonstrate threat, evaluate vulnerabilities and recommend countermeasures.

4. Discussion

Here appears the heart of the document. GONDOLA WISH is defined as a program to:

1. train military personnel in parapsychological collection

2. use that capability as a threat simulation or demonstration

3. detect what sensitive information could be exploited

4. close the OPSEC loop with countermeasures

This is key: the memo does not only talk about investigar psi, but also about operationalizing it as a network-team tool or hypothetical threat applied to security.

5. Conclusions

The conclusions are direct:

  • the Soviets would have this capability
  • the US military would be vulnerable
  • countermeasures are needed
  • the 902d MI Group would be the ideal organization

6. Action recommended

The final recommendation is to continue GONDOLA WISH, fund minimal training and adopt the project objectives plan.

What does the document hold, clean?

Strong institutional claim

The memo treats it as plausible that intelligence by paranormal means can exist and have operational consequences.

Striking historical claim

It claims that the CIA used parapsychological techniques for offensive intelligence in the early 1970s.

An operative claim

It suggests that training your own personnel would allow you to demonstrate real vulnerabilities and design OPSEC countermeasures.

Why it matters within X Files

  • It is a bridge piece between remote viewing as folklore and remote viewing as bureaucratic-military language
  • shows that the topic was not only circulating in laboratories or dissemination, but also in institutional planning.
  • helps understand how something like cia-mars-exploration-1984 then becomes imaginable within the Stargate ecosystem
  • connects directly with the documentary and testimonial line of the Stargate trilogy

Critical reading

What it does show

  • that the document exists and is authentic as an archival piece
  • that part of the intelligence/defense apparatus spoke about the issue in operational terms
  • that the institutional language around psi was considerably more serious than the pop stereotype suggests

What it does not prove

  • does not prove that remote sensing worked
  • does not validate assumptions about Soviet superiority
  • does not provide reproducible experimental results
  • does not demonstrate that CIA or SRI had obtained usable intelligence in a robust and repeatable way

How to use it

Read GONDOLA WISH as historical evidence of institutional belief, strategic framing, and bureaucratic priorities. Do not use it as empirical proof of paranormal abilities.
