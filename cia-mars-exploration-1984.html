CIA Mars Exploration 1984

Summary

Declassified document associated with the remote viewing / Stargate universe. Records a 1984-05-22 session in which a subject describes perceptions about Mars at an approximate time of 1 million years ago, using coordinates provided by a monitor. The strong value of the document is not evidential but historical: it shows how this type of sessions was archived within the American intelligence documentary ecosystem.

What does it actually contain?

  • acquisition method: sealed envelope + geographic coordinates
  • declared target: The planet Mars, time of interest approximately 1 million years B.C.
  • Complete guided transcription between monitor (MON) and subject (SUB)
  • descriptions of pyramids, megalithic structures, carved canyons, obelisks, canals, and shelters
  • appearance of tall, thin humanoid entities, described as a civilization in environmental collapse
  • final narrative about migration to another place and a catastrophe on a planetary/cosmic scale

Why it matters within X Files

  • joins CIA + Stargate + remote viewing + ancient Mars into a single parent document
  • is one of the most cited pieces of remote viewing documentary folklore
  • serves as a base node to connect with @@PH0@@ and future notes on Joseph McMoneagle, Monroe Institute and Project Stargate
  • allows you to separate three layers that are usually mixed: document authenticity, program existence, empirical validity of the claims

Story structure

1) Opening of the target

The monitor delivers coordinates under a blind protocol and the subject immediately reports a yellowish pyramidal shape in a depressed area.

2) Before and after the geological event

The subject claims to perceive a post-catastrophe Mars covered by clouds/dust storms and then regresses to a previous moment, where smooth surfaces, angular walls and megalithic constructions appear.

3) Presence of beings

"Shadows" of very tall and thin people appear. The session then insists that they are ancient beings, dressed in tight clothing, who would be waiting for a solution while their environment collapses.

4) Architecture and landscape

The session goes through several points and repeats patterns:

  • pyramids in depressed areas
  • Huge structures carved into canyon walls
  • honeycomb / rabbit warren interior spaces
  • an obelisk comparable to the Washington Monument
  • half-buried squares that reflect light
  • deep canals or aqueduct-like channels

5) Shelters and collapse

In the more narrative part, the pyramids appear as shelters against extreme storms. The subject describes functional chambers, hibernation/sleep during storms, and a population awaiting the return of a group sent to find another habitable place.

6) Migration

The session ends with the idea of ​​an exodus: some would have left in a ship or closed metal structure towards another world or area with volcanoes, gases and vegetation, also hostile.

Critical reading

What it does show

  • that this text was archived and declassified
  • that there was bureaucratic/operational interest in recording remote viewing sessions
  • that the document is part of the authentic cultural corpus of Stargate

What it does not prove

  • that there were ancient Martian civilizations
  • that the coordinates correspond to real structures
  • that the content of the session has scientific validation

Useful interpretation

The best way to read this piece is as a combination of:

1. royal historical document

2. Paranormal Cold War Narrative Artifact

3. central input of the modern conspiratorial imaginary about Mars, lost civilizations and psi programs
