Aim

Follow latest-ufo-sightings.net as a secondary source/aggregator of UFO-UAP content to spot new articles worth referring to X Files.

Critical reading

  • Useful source for narrative discovery and monitoring
  • do not treat as primary evidence
  • separate sightings, whistleblower claims, folklore, clickbait and coverage from real documents
  • prioritize posts connected to documents, historical incidents, identifiable witnesses or intersections with primary sources
