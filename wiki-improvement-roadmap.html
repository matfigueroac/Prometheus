Wiki Improvement Roadmap

Aim

Elevate the vault from useful notes into a maintained knowledge and publishing system. The standard is simple: important notes should become serious references with context, structure, critical reading, source separation and useful links.

Current operating phase

Active phase: Prometheus system hardening.

Purpose:

  • make the operating method explicit,
  • keep Telegram quiet and useful,
  • preserve a single task source of truth,
  • make X Files publication verifiable,
  • reduce dependence on saturated Hermes memories.

Control note: Prometheus System Operating Contract (private operating note).

Content lane now

Active content lane: official UFO/UAP + alternative archaeology.

Next strongest candidates:

  • latest-ufo-sightings-watch

  • ancient-civilizations-and-theories

Objective quality criterion

A mature vault note should have, when applicable:

  • consistent frontmatter,
  • brief but precise summary,
  • what it actually contains,
  • why it matters,
  • source separation,
  • evidence strength,
  • critical reading,
  • related navigation,
  • next improvement if unfinished.
