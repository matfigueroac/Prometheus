How to read the archive

Each dossier distinguishes four kinds of material.

  • primary sources,
  • secondary sources,
  • interpretation,
  • speculation.

For a concrete example, compare the declassified Mars session with the Stargate films.

Editorial method: X Files Dossier Method

Quality criteria

Use these questions when comparing two accounts.

  • what it actually contains,
  • why it matters,
  • evidence strength,
  • critical reading,
  • primary vs secondary sources,
  • related notes for contrast.
